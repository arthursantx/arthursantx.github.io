NEXUS — Strategic Technology Management Platform

A strategic management platform that connects business needs, technology investments and executive decisions.

NEXUS in 90 seconds

Presentation with Portuguese and English subtitles on screen and an instrumental soundtrack.

01Context

In many companies, technology requests arrive through different channels: an email from sales, a spreadsheet from finance, an urgent request from operations. Each team sees its own problem, but leadership rarely has a single view of everything being requested, what it costs and what has already been decided.

02The problem

Without a shared place, basic management questions are hard to answer:

  • Which business problems need to be solved first?
  • How much does the company invest in technology, and where?
  • What return is expected from each initiative, and is it happening?
  • Which projects are at risk, and which decisions has leadership already made?

03Proposed solution

NEXUS organizes the full journey: the need starts in a team, is assessed against clear criteria, becomes a portfolio initiative, receives a recorded decision and, once delivered, has its results tracked.

Prioritization uses a matrix with configurable weights (strategic alignment, financial impact, operational impact, urgency and feasibility). The score supports the conversation; it does not replace the decision.

04Goals

  • Give leadership a single view of requests, investments and risks.
  • Make prioritization transparent and explainable.
  • Record decisions with rationale, owners and next steps.
  • Check whether the value promised by each initiative was actually delivered.

05Features

01Executive Overview

KPIs calculated from the recorded data, plus automatic highlights.

02Business Needs

A record of each team’s needs, with impact, urgency and history.

03Strategic Prioritization

Weighted 0–100 matrix and an impact × effort view.

04Technology Portfolio

Initiatives with budget, owners, risks and timeline.

05NEXUS Intelligence

Answers questions using only the platform data and says when data is missing. In phase 1 it is rule-based, without generative AI.

06Executive Decisions

Decision Log with type, rationale, participants and deadlines.

07Reports & Insights

Reports by period and team, with PDF and CSV export.

08Value Tracking

Compares projected and measured savings, separating estimated from confirmed benefits.

06My contribution

  • Product concept and value proposition.
  • Mapping business problems and personas.
  • Defining the modules, requirements and business rules, such as the prioritization criteria and weights and how delivered value is calculated.
  • Functional direction and validation of deliverables.

The technical implementation was done with the support of artificial intelligence, under my product direction. This division of roles is documented in the repository.

07Technologies

Phase 1 (published): React, TypeScript and Vite, charts with Recharts, tests with Vitest and automatic publishing to GitHub Pages through GitHub Actions. Business rules are kept separate from the screens and have their own tests.

Phase 2 (planned): an API with a PostgreSQL database, real login with server-side permissions, and generative AI configured on the server.

08Results and learnings

Phase 1 is published as a demo, with data from a fictional company. Every module works in the browser, with access profiles, an audit trail and report export.

  • A prioritization rule is only convincing when it can be explained: that is why the screen shows how much each criterion added.
  • Estimated and confirmed benefits need to be shown separately, and hours saved are not money saved.
  • Documenting the vision, processes, rules and decisions before building made the implementation faster and easier to review.
