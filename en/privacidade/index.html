Privacy and terms of use

How this website handles personal data, security and copyright. Short version: none of your data is collected.

01Who is responsible

This website belongs to Arthur Borges dos Santos, who is responsible for its content and for the data handling described here. For any questions, write to arthurborgess21@gmail.com.

02Data the website collects

None. The website is static: it has no login, database, tracking cookies, visitor analytics or ads.

03Contact form

When you submit the form, your own email app opens with the message filled in. Nothing is sent or stored by this website; the message only leaves when you send it from your email.

04Preferences saved in your browser

Your accessibility panel choices are stored only in your browser (local storage), so you do not need to set them again. The NEXUS demo also keeps the fictional data you change, the language and your accessibility preferences in your browser. None of this is sent to me. To erase it, just clear the site data in your browser settings.

05Third-party services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical access data such as your IP address under GitHub’s privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which makes your browser connect to Google’s servers. The Brazilian Sign Language translator (VLibras, from the Brazilian Federal Government, served through the jsDelivr network) only loads if you turn it on. Links to LinkedIn and GitHub lead to sites with their own policies.

06Your rights (LGPD, Brazil’s data protection law)

Since the website stores no personal data, there is no record to access, correct or delete. If you email me, the message stays only in my inbox and can be deleted at your request.

08Security

Because it is static, the website stores no passwords or visitor data. It uses a secure connection (HTTPS) and a content security policy that only allows resources from trusted sources. If you find a vulnerability, write to the email above; the contact is also in security.txt.
